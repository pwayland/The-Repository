INTERMETRO Diagnostics
11 / 19Three Signs of a Bad Prompt
Three signs of a bad prompt

If the answer is this, your prompt is that.

i.
Generic output — you forgot context about your institution. Fix: add who you are, what you know, what's true of your students.
ii.
Unusable output — you forgot constraints (budget, timeline, resources). Fix: "Implementable this year, no new budget, ≤ 90 days."
iii.
Consultant-speak — you asked for "best practices" instead of solving a real problem. Fix: describe a specific decision you need to make on Monday.

18 / 19AI Burnout — Catching-Up
AI Burnout · Type 1 — The catching-up trap

The stages of falling behind.

Awarenessthat something important is changing.
Effortto keep up — reading, experimenting, attending workshops.
Recognitionthat the gap between what you know and what exists is widening despite the effort.
Dreadthat falling behind carries professional consequences.
Exhaustionfrom the effort coupled with the sense of futility. Acceptance is the way out.

AI Burnout · Type 2 — The strategic-thinking trap

The feedback loop that eats your day.

Leveragebetter thinking produces better AI output.
Reinforcementbetter output confirms the value of investing more thought.
Compulsionthe high return on thinking makes it addictive.
Disappearance30 minutes becomes 4 hours. A workday in continuous strategic thinking is more cognitively demanding than most people expect.

## Common Policies Across Calendars

### Refund Schedule (Partial Withdrawal)
- **Before classes begin:** 100% refund of tuition (fees not included for individual courses; fees included for total withdrawal)
- **First ~1 week of classes:** 75% refund
- **Second ~1 week:** 50% refund
- After the 50% date: No refund for partial withdrawals (see institutional Return of Funds policy for total withdrawals — `costs-financial-aid.md`)

### Financial Aid Disbursement Rhythm
After each calendar's listed accreditation/refund date: "accreditation every Tuesday and Thursday, and Friday there will be an accreditation and Refund Process until June 19, 2026. Check delivery to students will be made not later than three (3) days after each Reimbursement Process."

## V. Cambios en los Cargos y Cuotas

The University reserves the right to revise charges and fees when:

1. There is an increase in Educational and General Expenses or Mandatory Transfers
2. Budget projections indicate a possible increase in these expenses
3. After careful analysis of any special situation, University administration determines changes are reasonable and justified

## VI. Pagaré Único (Single Promissory Note)

1. The Pagaré Único is the document that guarantees payment of debts to UIPR.
2. Every bonafide registered student must complete and submit the Pagaré Único at the Oficina de Recaudaciones of their academic unit.
3. Students under 21 must sign with a parent/guardian. Students 21+ may sign without parental consent.

---

## VII. Pagos (Payments)

- Tuition and related charges are due **in full at the moment of registration** (matrícula).
- The difference between total cost (tuition, fees, other charges) and financial aid received (except Work-Study) is payable at registration.
- **Accepted payment methods:**
  - Money orders (giros)
  - Checks (payable to Universidad Interamericana de Puerto Rico)
  - Cash
  - Credit cards: MasterCard, VISA, American Express, Discover
  - ATH debit card
  - Web payment: MasterCard and VISA

---

## VIII. Arreglos de Pagos Diferidos (Deferred Payment Arrangements)

The University offers students the privilege of deferring **75% of total matrícula cost** for the semester, trimester, or bimestre, via signed Prórroga Pagaré Único.

**Eligibility:** Student must have settled any debt from prior academic terms. Total deferred cannot exceed balance after subtracting financial aid and loans.

**Exception:** The chief executive of the unit may increase the deferred percentage in exceptional cases if there is reasonable certainty of collection.

### Payment Schedule for Deferred Amounts

| Term | Deferred amount due | Number of installments |
|------|---------------------|------------------------|
| Semestre | 75 days after first day of classes | Up to 3 equal installments |
| Trimestre | 45 days after first day of classes | Up to 2 equal installments |
| Bimestre | 30 days after first day of classes | 1 installment |

- **Deferred payment fee:** $6.00 (see §IV)
- **Late installment fee:** $25.00 per unsatisfied installment
- **Verano:** NO deferred payment plan during summer sessions, except by express authorization of the Vicepresidente de Gerencia, Finanzas y Servicios Sistémicos. When authorized, payment must be satisfied within 30 days from the last class day.

Students are personally responsible for tracking due dates and paying on time.

### Total Withdrawal (from SmartCatalog policy)

For students who completely withdraw, cease attendance, never attend, or are expelled:

| Scenario | Refund |
|----------|--------|

## X. Licencias y Bajas Militares (Military Leave and Withdrawal)

### A. Refunds for Total Withdrawal

If a student is called to active service or activated due to national emergency and presents official evidence, the institution makes every effort to help the student benefit from available study programs to avoid withdrawal. Withdrawals are a last resort.

For total withdrawals, refunds follow **Title IV and Military Tuition Assistance (TA)** provisions.

The schedule for returning unearned military assistance funds is based on a daily proportional calculation up to **60%**. Formula:

> **(Days completed) / (Total days in term) = % earned**

Refund is calculated if the percentage earned is less than 60%.

**Example calculations:**

| Término académico | Total días | Días devengados | % no devengado | Comentario |
|-------------------|-----------:|----------------:|---------------:|------------|
| Semestre | 111 | 66 | 40% | 60% del curso completado |
| Trimestre | 90 | 54 | 40% | 60% del curso completado |
| Bimestre | 65 | 39 | 40% | 60% del curso completado |
| Intensivo | 11 | 7 | 40% | 60% del curso completado |
| Verano | 21 | 13 | 40% | 60% del curso completado |

### B. Per-Course Refund — Military Tuition Assistance Policy

If a servicemember using military tuition assistance withdraws from a course before completing 60% of the period of study, the institution calculates the unearned military tuition assistance and returns those funds to the corresponding military service.

Formula (same as above):

> **(Days completed) / (Total days in term) = % earned**

No refund after 60% of the academic term.

# Financial Aid

## Application

- **FAFSA:** https://studentaid.gov (FSA ID required)
- Federal school codes range **003939 (Aguadilla)** to **G42510 (Optometría)** — confirm Metro's code with the Financial Aid Office.

## Federal Financial Aid

All programs require minimum **6 credits per term** at the undergraduate level.

### Federal Pell Grant
- Non-repayable grant
- Undergraduates with exceptional financial need, no bachelor's degree
- Expires upon completion of first bachelor's
- 6-year full-time enrollment limit (LEU 600%)

### Federal Supplemental Educational Opportunity Grant (FSEOG)
- Non-repayable
- Priority to Pell recipients with exceptional need

### Federal Direct Loans

| Year | Total annual limit | Subsidized portion |
|------|-------------------:|-------------------:|
| First-year dependent | $5,500 | $3,500 |
| Second-year dependent | $6,500 | $4,500 |
| Third-year+ dependent | $7,500 | $5,500 |

- **Subsidized:** government pays interest while in school (≥½ time) and during authorized deferment
- **Unsubsidized:** interest accrues immediately
- Independent students have higher annual limits

### Federal Work-Study (FWS)
- Part-time employment to cover educational expenses
- When possible, assignments relate to field of study
- Earnings paid directly to student

### Maximum Time Requirements
- Must complete program within **150%** of published duration
- Pell: **6 years** of full-time enrollment equivalent

## Key Restrictions

### Second-Major Rule
Students who opt for a **second major not within their academic program** are **NOT eligible** for:
- Federal Pell Grant
- FSEOG
- Some Commonwealth programs

### Veterans
Access benefits via **U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs**. UIPR is approved by Agencia Estatal de Servicios Educativos a Veteranos for GI Bill® programs. Campus Veterans Affairs offices coordinate certification.
